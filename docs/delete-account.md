# Delete your Enough account and data

**In the app:** More > Account and sync > **Delete my account and cloud data**. This deletes your
account and everything stored in the cloud, then empties the app on that phone. It cannot be undone.

**Without your old phone:** install Enough on any Android phone, choose **Continue with Google** with the
account you used before, then use the steps above. If you cannot do that, open an issue at
[github.com/tanim913/bluecoins/issues](https://github.com/tanim913/bluecoins/issues) titled "Delete my
Enough account" without private details, and the reply will explain how to confirm it is you.

Data that only lives on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
