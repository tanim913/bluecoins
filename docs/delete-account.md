# Delete your Bluecoins account and data

**In the app:** More > Account and sync > **Delete my account and cloud data**. This deletes your
account and everything stored in the cloud, then empties the app on that phone. It cannot be undone.

**Without the app:** email the contact address in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) from the
Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Bluecoins account". The account and
all its data are deleted within 30 days.

Data that only lives on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
